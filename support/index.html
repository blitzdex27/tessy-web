About Tessy

Tessy – Easy Sheets lays out your own pictures and text, or the starter pictures that come with it, on a page at the exact size you choose, fills the page with copies, and prints them true to scale with AirPrint, or shares them as a PDF or a picture, with cut guides to trim along. Tessy is developed by Dexter Ramos, an independent developer.

  • Free to download. Tessy is free to download, with no ads.
  • Private by design. Your pictures and sheets stay on your device and are never uploaded to us.
  • No account needed. You don't have to sign up or sign in.
  • No tracking. No analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Frequently Asked Questions

My printed pictures are the wrong size.

In your printer's options, turn off "fit to page", "scale to fit" or any other scaling, and print at 100%. To check your printer, go to Settings › Printing & Layout › Printer margins, tap Check a printer… and print the test page, then measure it with a real ruler. If you share a PDF and print it from another app, choose 100% or "actual size" there too.

How do I add pictures?

Inside a sheet, tap Add and choose Photos or Files. Photos opens Apple's photo picker, and Files lets you pick pictures from your device, iCloud Drive or other storage providers. You can also drag pictures onto the page from another app, on iPhone and iPad: hold them with one finger, switch to Tessy with another, and drop. You can paste a picture you've copied too. Tessy only sees the pictures you choose. No pictures to hand? Tap Add, then Starter Pictures.

What are the starter pictures, and can I use them?

They're pictures, gift tag art, label art and paper patterns that come with Tessy, so you can make a sheet straight away. Find them under Add › Starter Pictures, under Starter Pictures… when you add a ready-made piece, and under Paper › Background › Paper Patterns. They were made for Tessy with AI image generation or drawn by script, and don't show real people. You can use them in what you print with Tessy, including things you sell, with no credit or payment. You can't sell or give them away on their own. The Terms of Use have the details.

Which fonts can I use, and where are the credits?

Text boxes use a set of fonts that come with Tessy, so they look the same on screen, in PDFs and on paper. Each font is free for commercial use under the SIL Open Font License. You'll find every font's credits and full licence in Settings › Acknowledgements.

How do I delete a sheet, or get one back?

When you delete a sheet, it moves to Recently Deleted. You can restore it from there for 30 days, or erase it permanently at any time. After 30 days it's removed automatically. Pictures that no sheet uses any more are cleaned up too. Uninstalling Tessy removes all data the app stored on your device.

Is Tessy free?

Yes. This version is free, with no purchases, subscriptions or ads. If that changes, the App Store listing will say so.

Are my pictures uploaded anywhere?

No. Your pictures and sheets are stored only on your device, and we never receive a copy. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I need an account?

No. Tessy doesn't require you to create an account.

Which languages does Tessy support?

Tessy is available in English, Spanish and French. It follows your device's language, and you can choose a different one for Tessy in the Settings app under Apps › Tessy › Language.

Where is Tessy available?

Tessy is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad in several countries and regions. Check the App Store for availability in your region.
