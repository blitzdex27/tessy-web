Tessy Support

Need help with Tessy? Email dekideks_support@icloud.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

  • Will my photo be accepted? Tessy checks your photo against the published rules for the ID you pick, but only the issuing authority decides. Check their current requirements before you submit.
  • Can I use a digital outfit for a passport or visa? Many authorities reject digitally altered photos. Tessy warns you on those ID types; take the photo in suitable clothing instead.
  • My printed photo is the wrong size. In your printer settings, turn off "fit to page" or scaling and print at 100%. Tessy's printer setup, on the print options for a photo, can print a ruler test page to check your printer.
  • I deleted a photo by mistake. Open Recently Deleted. Photos stay there for 30 days.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Tap Restore Purchases in Settings, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.
  • How do I cancel Tessy Pro? Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Tessy.
  • Does Tessy upload my photos? No. Everything happens on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

When you email us

Tell us your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. Please don't send photos of your ID documents.
