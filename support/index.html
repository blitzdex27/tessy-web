Tessy Support

Need help with Tessy? We're happy to assist with questions, bug reports, and feature requests.

Contact Us

Email dekideks_support@icloud.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can. If you're reporting a problem, please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All photo processing happens on your device.

Will my photo be accepted by the authority?

Tessy checks your photo against published requirements as a best effort, but can't guarantee acceptance. Please confirm the requirements with the authority you're submitting to.

How do I cancel my Tessy Pro subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select Tessy.

How do I restore my purchases?

Use the Restore Purchases option in the app while signed in to the Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
