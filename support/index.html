About Tessy

Tessy helps you make ID photos and everyday headshots: choose a common or custom size, capture or pick a photo, frame it, review photo checks and optionally replace a general-size photo’s background, and arrange a print sheet. Tessy is developed by Dexter Ramos, an independent developer.

  • Free to use. Tessy is currently completely free, with no purchases or subscriptions.
  • Private by design. Photos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to us.
  • No account needed. You don't have to sign up or sign in.
  • No tracking. No advertising or cross-app tracking. Purchase-status services are described in the Privacy Policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tessy really free?

Yes. All features are free in Tessy 1.1.0. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions in this version.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

Tessy does not upload your photos to us. Photo processing and face analysis run on your device. Sharing, exported copies and device backups follow the options you choose. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I need an account?

No. Tessy doesn't require you to create an account.

Will my photo be accepted by the authority?

Tessy provides framing guidance and selected photo checks. These cannot verify every requirement or guarantee acceptance. Confirm the requirements with the organization receiving your photo.

Does Tessy change how I look?

Tessy doesn't change how you look. It frames and sizes your original photo, provides photo checks, and helps you arrange and print copies. Background removal and replacement are available for general 1 × 1, 2 × 2 and custom-size photos marked for non-official use.

Why are background tools unavailable on my photo?

Passport, visa and ICAO presets keep the original background, with no override. The same restriction applies if you mark a general-size photo for a passport, visa or government ID. Take the photo against a plain, light background that meets your document's requirements. You can adjust framing and print size.

What happens when I switch to an official preset?

Tessy rebuilds from the original photo and clears background changes. It keeps your original appearance and background. Follow the receiving organization's requirements.

Why can't I share or print an older saved photo?

Older saved photos with appearance changes remain in your library. Open Adjust photo to preview a clean version rebuilt from the original, with framing and any allowed background. Save replaces the saved photo; Cancel keeps it. Sharing and printing, including print sheets and Custom Layout, are unavailable until the clean version is saved. If the original cannot be read, take or choose the original again to create a new photo; the older saved copy stays unchanged.

My printed photo is the wrong size.

In your printer's options, turn off "fit to page" or scaling and print at 100%. Tessy's Printer Setup can print a ruler test page so you can check your printer's scale.

How do I delete a photo, or get one back?

When you delete a photo in Tessy, it moves to Recently Deleted. You can restore it from there for 30 days, or erase it permanently at any time. After 30 days, Tessy removes it when it next clears expired deleted photos. Copies you exported or backed up are managed separately. Uninstalling Tessy removes all data the app stored on your device.

Where is Tessy available?

Tessy supports iPhone and iPad, in English, Spanish and French. App Store availability depends on your region and the app’s release status.

Which languages does Tessy support?

Tessy supports English, Spanish and French.
