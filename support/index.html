About Tessy

Tessy helps you create ID, passport, and visa-style photos from a photo you take or choose. It crops to standard sizes, checks the photo against the rules for the ID you pick, replaces the background, retouches, and exports the result for printing or sharing. Tessy is developed by Dexter Ramos, an independent developer.

  • Free to use. Tessy is currently completely free, with no purchases or subscriptions.
  • Private by design. Photos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to us.
  • No account needed. You don't have to sign up or sign in.
  • No tracking. No analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tessy really free?

Yes. All features are currently free. If paid features are added in the future, this page will be updated.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All photo processing, including the face analysis Tessy uses to check your photo, happens on your device. Your photos are stored locally in the app, and we never receive a copy. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I need an account?

No. Tessy doesn't require you to create an account.

Will my photo be accepted by the authority?

Tessy checks your photo against the published requirements for the ID you pick, but only the issuing authority decides. Requirements vary by country and change over time, so please confirm them with the authority you're submitting to.

Can I use a digital outfit or retouching for a passport or visa?

Many authorities reject digitally altered photos. Tessy locks outfits and retouching for passport and visa IDs by default and warns you if you turn them on. For those documents, take the photo in suitable clothing instead.

My printed photo is the wrong size.

In your printer's options, turn off "fit to page" or scaling and print at 100%. Tessy's Printer Setup can print a ruler test page so you can check your printer's scale.

How do I delete a photo, or get one back?

When you delete a photo in Tessy, it moves to Recently Deleted. You can restore it from there for 30 days, or erase it permanently at any time. After 30 days it's removed automatically. Uninstalling Tessy removes all data the app stored on your device.

Where is Tessy available?

Tessy is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad in several countries and regions, in English, Spanish, and French. Check the App Store for availability in your region.
