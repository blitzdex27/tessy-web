Contact Us

Email tessy_support@dekideks.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can. If you're reporting a problem, please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

About Tessy

Tessy helps you create ID, passport, and visa-style photos from a photo you take or choose. It crops to standard sizes, replaces the background, retouches, and exports the result for printing or sharing. Tessy is developed by Dexter Ramos, an independent developer.

  • Free to use. Tessy is currently completely free, with no purchases or subscriptions.
  • Private by design. Photos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to us.
  • No account needed. You don't have to sign up or sign in.
  • No tracking. No analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tessy really free?

Yes. All features are currently free. If paid features are added in the future, this page will be updated.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All photo processing happens on your device. Your photos are stored locally in the app, and we never receive a copy.

Do I need an account?

No. Tessy doesn't require you to create an account.

Will my photo be accepted by the authority?

Tessy checks your photo against published requirements as a best effort, but can't guarantee acceptance. Requirements can vary by country and change over time, so please confirm them with the authority you're submitting to.

How do I delete my photos?

Delete a photo inside Tessy to remove it from the app's local database. Uninstalling Tessy removes all data the app stored on your device.

Where is Tessy available?

Tessy is available on the App Store in many countries and regions. Check the App Store listing for availability in your region.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

We'd love to hear it. Email tessy_support@dekideks.com with your device model, iOS version, and what happened.
