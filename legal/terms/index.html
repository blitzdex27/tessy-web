The Service

Tessy lets you lay out your own pictures and text on a page at the sizes you choose, fill the page with copies, and print the page or save it as a PDF, with optional cut guides. Your pictures and sheets are stored on your device.

Printed Size

Tessy draws each sheet at true scale, but the size that comes out on paper also depends on your printer, its settings and the paper you use. Printers can scale a page or shift it slightly, and some can't print all the way to the edge. Print at 100% with scaling turned off, and use the ruler test page in Printer Setup to check your printer before printing something important. We can't guarantee the printed size or quality of any page.
