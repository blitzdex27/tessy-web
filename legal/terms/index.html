Printed Size

Tessy draws each sheet at true scale, but the size that comes out on paper also depends on your printer, its settings and the paper you use. Printers can scale a page or shift it slightly, and some can't print all the way to the edge. Print at 100% with scaling turned off, and use Check a printer… in Settings › Printing & Layout › Printer margins to check your printer before printing something important.
