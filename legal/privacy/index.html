Tessy Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026

The short version: Tessy processes your photos entirely on your device. We don't run any servers, we don't collect your photos, and we don't track you.

Tessy ("the app," "we," "us") is developed by Dexter Ramos, an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains what happens to your information when you use Tessy.

Information We Collect

  • Photos you provide. When you take or select a photo to create an ID or passport photo, it's processed entirely on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks. It's stored locally in the app's database on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any server we operate — because we don't operate one.
  • Purchase information. Tessy is currently free and doesn't collect any purchase information. If we add paid features in the future, purchases will be handled by the App Store and may involve a subscription management provider, and this policy will be updated before that happens.
  • We don't collect your name, email address, or other personal information directly, and Tessy doesn't require you to create an account.

How We Use Information

The information above is used only to provide the app's core functionality — cropping, background removal, retouching, and printing or exporting your photos. We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell or share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data Storage and Your Control

Your photos and app data are stored locally on your device. Deleting a photo within Tessy removes it from the app's local database. Uninstalling Tessy removes all data the app stored on your device.

Third-Party Services

Tessy currently doesn't use any third-party services that receive your data. If that changes, we'll list them here.

We don't use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Tessy isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from children. Because the app doesn't collect personal information in the first place, no such data is retained regardless of the user's age.

International Users

Tessy is currently available on the Philippines App Store. If availability changes, this policy will be updated to reflect any additional considerations for other regions.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date at the top of this page.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to tessy_support@dekideks.com.
