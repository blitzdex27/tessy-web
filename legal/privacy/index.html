Tessy Privacy Policy

Effective [date of publication]

The short version: the pictures and text you put on your sheets stay on your device. There's no account, we don't run any servers, we never receive your pictures, and we don't track you.

Tessy – Easy Sheets ("Tessy," "the app," "we," "us") is developed by Dexter Ramos, an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains what happens to your information when you use Tessy.

Information We Collect

  • Pictures and text you add. When you add a picture to a sheet, Tessy keeps a resized copy of it in the app's database on your device, with location, camera and date details removed. Your sheets, including any text you type and the fonts and sizes you choose, are stored there too. None of this is sent to us or to any server we operate, because we don't operate one.
  • Purchase information. Tessy uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service, to check which in-app purchases are active. RevenueCat runs whenever the app is open, even while every feature is free. It receives an anonymous app user identifier that it creates, your purchase and subscription status, and basic technical details that come with any internet request, such as the app and iOS version, your App Store country and your IP address. Payments are processed by Apple. Neither we nor RevenueCat ever see your card details.
  • We don't collect your name, email address, or other personal information, and Tessy doesn't require you to create an account.

How Pictures Get Into Tessy

  • Photos. You choose pictures with Apple's photo picker, so Tessy only sees the pictures you pick. It doesn't need access to your photo library to do this.
  • Files. You choose pictures with Apple's Files picker, from your device, iCloud Drive or another storage provider you've set up. Tessy only sees the files you pick.
  • Drag and drop, and paste. Tessy only receives a picture when you drop it into a sheet or choose to paste it.

Tessy doesn't use the camera. If you save a sheet to your photo library, Tessy asks for permission to add to it; that permission doesn't let Tessy see your other photos. You can change this permission at any time in the iPhone or iPad Settings app.

How We Use Information

The information above is used only to provide the app's features (laying out, sizing, printing and exporting your sheets) and to verify and manage purchases where applicable. We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell or share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data Storage and Your Control

Your pictures and sheets are stored only on your device, in the app's local database. Device backups, such as iCloud Backup, may include this data, as they do for most apps.

When you delete a sheet, it moves to Recently Deleted, where you can restore it for 30 days. After 30 days it's permanently removed. You can also erase it from Recently Deleted at any time. Pictures that no sheet uses any more are removed automatically. Uninstalling Tessy removes all data the app stored on your device.

When you print, share or save a PDF, the sheet goes only where you send it, such as your printer, another app or the Files app.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple (App Store) processes payments for any purchase as the merchant of record. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat manages purchase status, as described above. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

The fonts in Tessy come with the app and are never downloaded. We don't use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as access, correction, or deletion. We don't hold your pictures or any account information, so your pictures remain on your device under your control. RevenueCat's purchase records use an anonymous identifier that we can't link to you by name. If you have a question or request, contact us at tessy.support@dekideks.com.

Children's Privacy

Tessy isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

International Users

Tessy is available on the App Store in several countries and regions. Your pictures and sheets are stored only on your device. Purchase status handled by RevenueCat may be processed in the United States.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date at the top of this page.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to tessy.support@dekideks.com.
