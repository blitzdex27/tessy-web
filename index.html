Tessy

Print it at its real size.

Tessy – Easy Sheets puts your pictures and text on a page at the exact size you choose, then prints them true to scale. Make gift tags, photo strips, labels and sheets of your favourite pictures, ready to cut.

From picture to printed sheet in four steps

  1. 1. Start a sheet

    Pick a paper size, such as Letter, A4 or 4 × 6, or start with ready-made gift tags, instant photos or photo strips.

  2. 2. Add pictures

    Bring them in from Photos or Files, drag them straight onto the page, or paste them.

  3. 3. Set the size

    Type a size in inches, centimetres or millimetres, or drag the handles. Add text, shapes and arrows if you like.

  4. 4. Print

    Fill the page with copies, then print at 100%, or share it as a PDF or a picture.

Why Tessy exists.

Tessy started with my mom. She wanted her pictures to come out of the printer at the size she asked for, and it was surprisingly hard. So I built an app that prints what you see, at the size you chose.

Exact sizes, not guesses.

Every box on the page has a real size. Type one in inches, centimetres or millimetres, or drag the handles and watch it update. Rulers along the page and distance labels between boxes show exactly where everything sits.

Ready-made pieces.

Choose gift tags, instant photos or photo strips, pick your pictures, and the page fills up. Everything stays editable, and cut guides go around each piece.

A full page, in one tap.

Fill the page with copies of a picture, or arrange different ones side by side. Boxes snap to each other and to the printable edge, and Align and Space Evenly keep rows straight.

Make it yours.

Crop like you do in Photos, then round the corners or add a border. Add text in hand-picked fonts, shapes, arrows and a page background.

Layers you can see.

Put text over a picture or tuck one behind another. Reorder everything in the Layers list, group things that belong together, lock what shouldn't move, or look at the stack in 3D.

Prints true to size.

Print with AirPrint, or share a PDF or a picture. Cut guides show where to trim, and Tessy remembers each printer's margins so nothing gets cut off. A test page lets you check your printer before you use good paper.

Your sheets, kept safe.

Every sheet stays on your device, ready to edit or print again. Deleted one by mistake? It waits in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
