Tessy

Print it at its real size.

Tessy – Easy Sheets puts your pictures and text on a page at the exact size you choose, fills the page with copies, and prints them true to scale, ready to cut.

From picture to printed sheet in four steps

  1. 1. Start a sheet

    Pick a paper size, such as Letter, A4 or 4 × 6, in portrait or landscape.

  2. 2. Add pictures

    Bring them in from Photos or Files, drag them in, or paste them.

  3. 3. Set the size

    Choose 1 × 1 in, 2 × 2 in or a custom size, or drag the handles. Add text if you like.

  4. 4. Print

    Fill the page with copies, then print at 100% or save a PDF.

Why Tessy exists.

Tessy started with my mom. She needed a few small pictures printed at an exact size, lots to a page, ready to cut, and getting them to come out at the right size was surprisingly hard. So I built an app that prints what you see, at the size you asked for.

Exact sizes, not guesses.

Every picture box has a real size in inches. Pick 1 × 1 in or 2 × 2 in, type a custom size, or drag the handles and watch the size update as it snaps to the nearest eighth of an inch.

A full page, in one tap.

Fill the page with copies of a picture, or arrange different ones side by side. Pictures snap to each other, to the page's centre lines and to the printable edge, so rows stay straight.

Words on the page.

Add text boxes for names, dates or captions. Choose from a set of hand-picked fonts that come with the app, then set the size, colour and alignment.

Simple layers.

Put text over a picture or tuck one picture behind another. Bring forward, send backward, or reorder everything in the Layers list.

Prints true to size.

Print with AirPrint or save a PDF. Cut guides show where to trim, and a ruler test page lets you check that your printer prints at actual size before you use good paper.

Your sheets, kept safe.

Every sheet stays on your device, ready to edit or print again. Deleted one by mistake? It waits in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
