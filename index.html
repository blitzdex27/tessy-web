Tessy

Print it at its real size.

Tessy – Easy Sheets puts your pictures and text on a page at the exact size you choose, then prints them true to scale. Make gift tags, labels, cards, photo strips and sheets of your favourite pictures, ready to cut.

From picture to printed sheet in four steps

  1. 1. Start a sheet

    Pick what you're making on Home, such as photos, gift tags, labels, cards or name tags, or start with a blank page in Letter, A4, 4 × 6 or another paper size.

  2. 2. Add pictures

    Bring them in from Photos or Files, drag them straight onto the page, or paste them. Nothing to hand? Starter pictures come with the app.

  3. 3. Set the size

    Type a size in inches, centimetres or millimetres, or drag the handles. A template fills in like a form. Add text, shapes and arrows if you like.

  4. 4. Print

    Fill the page with copies. Ready to Print says which paper to load, then prints at 100% with AirPrint. Or share a PDF or a picture.

Why Tessy exists.

Tessy started with my mom. She wanted her pictures to come out of the printer at the size she asked for, and it was surprisingly hard. So I built an app that prints what you see, at the size you chose.

Exact sizes, not guesses.

Every box on the page has a real size. Type one in inches, centimetres or millimetres, or drag the handles and watch it update. Rulers along the page and distance labels between boxes show exactly where everything sits.

Templates that fill in like a form.

Choose from 18 templates, from instant photos, photo strips and gift tags to labels, party invitations, bookmarks and recipe cards. Type the words and pick your pictures, or the art that comes with Tessy, and long names get smaller to fit. Everything stays editable, and you can save your own templates and share them as files.

One for each name.

Type or paste a list, choose people from Contacts, or open a CSV or text file, and Tessy makes a name tag, label or gift tag for each one. A sheet can have pages, and Tessy adds them as it needs them.

A full page, in one tap.

Fill the page with copies of a picture, or arrange different ones side by side. Boxes snap to each other and to the printable edge, and Line Up and Space Evenly keep rows straight.

Make it yours.

Crop, flip, turn and straighten a picture, then round the corners or add a border. Add text in hand-picked fonts, shapes, arrows and a page background, with paper patterns ready to use.

Layers you can see.

Put text over a picture or tuck one behind another. Reorder everything in the Layers list, group things that belong together, lock what shouldn't move, or look at the stack in 3D.

Prints true to size.

Print with AirPrint, or share a PDF or a picture. Choose the pages and copies, and cut guides show where to trim. The Printers tab remembers each printer’s margins to help you keep items inside the printable area, and prints a test page to check your printer before you use good paper.

Your sheets, kept safe.

Every sheet stays on your device, ready to edit or print again. Deleted one by mistake? It waits in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
