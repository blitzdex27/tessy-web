FROM PHOTO TO PAPER

Four steps. One place.

  1. 01

    Choose a size

    Start with 2 × 2 or 1 × 1 inches, or enter a custom width and height in millimeters.

  2. 02

    Take or pick a photo

    Use camera guidance for framing, or choose a photo from your library.

  3. 03

    Refine your headshot

    Adjust framing and choose optional background, outfit or retouching tools for a non-official photo.

  4. 04

    Print your sheet

    Fill a page with copies, arrange a layout, then print with AirPrint or export a PDF.

A LITTLE PHOTO STUDIO

The tools for the photo you need.

↔

Common and custom sizes

Make 1 × 1 and 2 × 2 photos, or choose dimensions for a school badge, company application or another everyday use. Photo output is sized at 300 dpi.

◐

A different background

For your non-official headshot, replace a distracting background with a solid color or another background you choose.

✧

An optional new look

Try suits and blazers for a résumé or profile headshot. Choose a cut, then move and resize the outfit to adjust its fit.

⌖

Retouch with control

Choose the touch-ups you want. Use Touch or Painter, zoom into details, and use Heal, Clone or Restore on a non-official photo.

▦

A sheet your way

Repeat a photo, mix photos on one page, or place them yourself in Custom Layout. Set the paper, margins and cut guides before printing.

▱

Ready for the next print

Keep photos and layouts together in Tessy. Make several photo sizes in one session, reopen a saved layout, or share a finished JPEG.
